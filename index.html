Program Overview 課程概覽

What is Pacific English Study Junior Program?
什麼是 Pacific English Study 青少年課程？

Pacific English Study Junior Program is a premium short-term English and activity program specifically designed for young learners aged 12-17. Our program combines intensive English language instruction with unforgettable Australian cultural experiences, adventure activities, and meaningful friendships with students from around the world.

Pacific English Study 青少年課程是專為 12-17 歲年輕學習者設計的高品質短期英語與活動課程。我們的課程結合密集英語教學與難忘的澳洲文化體驗、冒險活動，以及與來自世界各地學生建立有意義的友誼。

English Program 英語課程

🎓 Communicative English 溝通式英語

Communicative English with focus on speaking, listening, reading, and writing. Experienced ESL teachers deliver engaging, interactive lessons suited to Elementary to Intermediate levels. Level placement test on arrival; graduation certificate on completion.

著重聽說讀寫的溝通式英語教學。經驗豐富的英語教師提供適合初級至中級程度的互動式課程。抵達時進行分級測驗；完成後頒發畢業證書。

🌍 Immersive Experience 沉浸式體驗

Live with local Australian families, experience authentic culture, practice English daily, enjoy 3 meals per day in a supportive home environment. Our homestay families are carefully selected for their warmth, safety, and commitment to student wellbeing.

與當地澳洲家庭同住，體驗真實文化，每日練習英語，在支持性的家庭環境中享用每日三餐。我們的寄宿家庭經過仔細篩選，以其溫暖、安全和對學生福祉的承諾而著稱。

Frequently Asked Questions 常見問題

How much English do I need to know? 我需要具備多少英語能力？

Elementary to Intermediate level is required. We assess your level and place you in appropriate classes.

需要初級至中級程度。我們會評估您的程度並安排適合的班級。

What if I'm nervous about leaving home? 如果我對離家感到緊張怎麼辦？

Our experienced staff and caring host families are trained to support international students. We provide 24/7 support.

我們經驗豐富的工作人員與關懷的寄宿家庭都受過訓練，能支援國際學生。我們提供 24/7 支援。

Can I extend my stay? 我可以延長住宿嗎？

Yes! Additional homestay nights are available at $80 AUD per night.

可以！額外的寄宿家庭住宿每晚澳幣 $80。

Is travel insurance really required? 旅遊保險真的是強制性的嗎？

Yes, comprehensive travel insurance is mandatory for your protection.

是的，為了您的保護，綜合旅遊保險是強制性的。

What's the class size? 班級人數是多少？

Maximum 15 students per class, ensuring personalized attention.

每班最多 15 人，確保個別化關注。

Do you help with visa applications? 你們協助簽證申請嗎？

Yes! We provide letters of enrollment for visa applications.

是的！我們為簽證申請提供入學信函。
